Intelligence Layers

Each layer is independently deployable, horizontally scalable, and designed for fault isolation.

01

Graph Engine

A dynamic property graph that models every warehouse, carrier, route, port, and customs checkpoint as live nodes with weighted, time-varying edges. Supports multi-hop queries in milliseconds.

Neo4j / TigerGraph Real-time Streaming Dijkstra+
1M+Nodes
<5msQuery Time
02

Simulation Engine

Discrete-event simulation that runs thousands of scenario replicas per second. Models disruptions, weather events, demand spikes, and port congestion before they hit the real network.

Monte Carlo DES Engine Digital Twin
50KSimulations/s
±2%Error Margin
03

Optimization Core

Multi-objective solver that simultaneously minimizes cost, time, carbon footprint, and risk. Uses hybrid meta-heuristics — genetic algorithms combined with linear programming for Pareto-optimal solutions.

Genetic Algorithm OR-Tools Pareto Front
38%Cost Saved
4 objSimultaneous
04

Prediction Engine

Temporal ensemble of Transformer-based time-series models, trained on geopolitical events, commodity prices, seasonal patterns, and historical disruptions to forecast demand and risk 90 days ahead.

AI Agent Mesh

A coordinated swarm of autonomous agents — each specialized in carrier negotiation, customs clearance, demand sensing, and exception handling. They communicate via a shared event bus and self-reassign tasks dynamically.
